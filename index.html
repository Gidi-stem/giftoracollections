About Giftora Collections

Giftora Collections is an online retail store built around one simple idea: everyday essentials shouldn't feel like a chore to buy. We stock clothing, fashion accessories, shoes, bags, home appliances, household items and personal care products — all handpicked and sold at prices that respect your budget.

We're a small team, which means every order gets a personal touch. When you message us, you're talking to someone who actually knows what's in stock and can help you choose.

  • • Quality-checked products across every category we sell
  • • Simple ordering — browse, add to bag, checkout via WhatsApp or form
  • • Real support from a real person, not a call center
What's next for Giftora: we're growing beyond general merchandise into fashion lines, baked goods, restaurant services, gardening services and counseling services. Follow our social pages so you don't miss the launch.

Delivery coverage & timing

  • Lagos: 1–2 working days
  • Other states: 3–5 working days
  • Delivery fees depend on your location and are confirmed before payment
  • Pickup available for customers within Lagos, by arrangement

Payment & returns

  • Bank transfer or pay-on-delivery (Lagos only)
  • Full payment confirmed before dispatch for out-of-state orders
  • Items can be returned within 48 hours if damaged or incorrect
  • Please inspect your order at the point of delivery
